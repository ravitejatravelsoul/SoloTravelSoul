## 4. Travel compatibility and group expectations

Existing matching and onboarding were reused. Before this phase a traveler saw only a title and a destination before sending a request, and an organizer saw only a name and a message before approving. Now:

- **Trip page and request form** show destination, dates, group size and places taken, status, **budget** and **what the organizer expects** (two new optional organizer-written fields, 80 and 300 characters, filtered like other public text), the organizer's own shared answers (only if they chose to share them), and **how your answers compare**: shared, different and not provided, using the real answers, never a score or personality label. Missing answers read "Not provided" and are never a mismatch.
- **Organizer approval card** has "Shared answers" (one document read when opened) next to Approve/Reject. Approval stays the organizer's decision; nothing joins automatically; profiles are not forced public; compatibility is stated as not a safety measure.
- **No mandatory membership condition was added**, so nothing needed server-side enforcement. Capacity and "accepting requests" are the organizer's own settings and are enforced by the organizer's client (the rules let only the organizer write the count). The terms gate is a client gate (below). There is no "three matching traits" rule.
- Verified on devices: facts, organizer answers, comparison and the request form (emulator, v2), the organizer card (phone, v2), editing the new fields after members joined (phone, v2). Logic is covered by `tests/release/safety.cjs`.

## 5. Stranger safety and policy changes (owner and legal decisions needed)

Changes: one shared wording file (`packages/shared/src/policy.ts`) used by the Terms, the Safety screen, the acceptance screen and the join screens, with a test that compares them and rejects forbidden promises.

- **Added:** compatibility is not identity verification or a safety guarantee; meet in public first; talk through plans, budget and boundaries; tell someone you trust; do not send money or share sensitive documents; how to report, block and leave; the app is not an emergency service and not monitored around the clock; the platform's actual role (no identity or background checks, no insurance, travel services or emergency help).
- **Removed or corrected claims the app could not keep:** "we review all reports within 48 hours"; "rated 17+"; "all connections require host approval" (direct chats and group adds do not); "blocked users cannot find you in discovery" (they still can in lists); Terms 3 said content is shown only to you although public posts are shown to others; Privacy said photos are public links although current uploads are authenticated; Cloudflare was not named as a processor; join requests, groups and chats were not described. A blanket waiver was not added; the disclaimer keeps "nothing limits any right you have under applicable law".
- **Terms acceptance:** explicit checkbox at sign-up, stored (version and server time) in the user's own document, and required (a routed screen) before the app opens for any account without it, including existing ones. Declining signs out. Verified on the phone for the existing account (accept) and on the emulator (decline then accept); the sign-up checkbox is covered by tests only. Google Play's policy asks for acceptance before users can create content (<https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937>); Apple guideline 1.2 asks for reporting with "timely responses", blocking and published contact information (<https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#user-generated-content>). This gate is not enforced by the rules (a modified client could skip it).
- **Owner and legal decisions needed:** (1) legal review of all text and the exact wording of the platform's role and disclaimers; (2) the minimum age (Terms say 13; the old Safety footer said 17+; TripBFF's store listing is 18+); (3) company identity, governing law and jurisdiction (not invented); (4) a response-time commitment for reports (Apple asks for timely responses; the staging queue held reports 11 to 15 days old, all test data); (5) whether anyone may add you to a group chat by email without your consent; (6) retention of reports, and of chats once every participant has deleted their account (D20); (7) whether to offer any verification and what it would honestly mean.

### 11.3 Safety controls

- **Leave trip and leave group:** retries and a second tap racing the first are safe (the member document must still exist for the count to fall; a retry finds it gone and succeeds). Counters verified exact live on staging (trip 3 to 2; community group 2 to 1) and in the emulator rules suite; access ends (member document gone, chat messages unreadable). The organizer cannot leave (the app refuses). On the phone: Leave trip and Leave group (community) both worked.
- **Moderation of public trips and community groups:** `moderationStatus` visible/hidden/removed set only by a moderator with their own id. While not visible: left out of discovery and matching (client filter), no new requests and no approvals (rules), the owner cannot edit, delete or restore it, members can still leave and anyone can still report. Moderators restore from a new "Hidden" tab; "Suspended" tab lists suspensions with "Lift suspension" (moderators only, not for an account being deleted, not by a suspended moderator). Verified on the emulator (hide from a report, restore from the Hidden tab, suspend, lift) with the phone as the affected member.
- **Group chat invitations:** a group starts with only its creator; people are invited by e-mail address and join by accepting (recipient sees "Group invitations" in Chats). There is no account lookup, so the sender is not told whether an address has an account. Accept, decline, withdraw and expiry (7 days) verified; a declined or accepted invitation is final; direct adds, self-adds and accepting for someone else are refused (live and rules tests). The deletion Worker removes invitations the account sent or that were addressed to its e-mail.
- **Terms on the server:** creating posts, journals, comments, public trips, community groups, join requests, direct and group chats and messages, activity-feed entries and place reviews requires `users/{uid}.termsAcceptedVersion` equal to the current version (a test compares it with `TERMS_VERSION`). Acceptance, reporting, leaving, blocking, account deletion and profile text stay open. Profile text (name, bio, city) is not gated, because an account must create its profile before it can accept; edits to existing content are not gated.
- **Preference sharing and compatibility:** the existing consent, withdrawal, missing-answer and organizer-approval suites pass unchanged; at 2.0x the summary and join form show "Not provided" and never describe compatibility as verification or a guarantee.

## 12. Invitation security and final device checks (2026-10-20)

**Gap closed.** Invitations are addressed to an e-mail address, and the rules trusted `request.auth.token.email` without `email_verified`, so an unverified account could sign up with another person's address and read, decline or accept the invitation. The deletion Worker also looked up "invitations addressed to this account" using the profile and directory e-mail, which clients can write.

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Rules (`firestore.rules`) | New `verifiedEmail()` (`token.email_verified == true`). Reading, listing, declining and accepting an invitation as the invited person require it; the inviter keeps read and cancel with no verification. Accepting now also requires the inviter not to be suspended or deleting and the group to exist (declining stays possible). `joiningByInvite()` repeats the same checks on the group write. |
| Deletion Worker | `removeGroupInvites` deletes invitations the account **sent**, and those addressed to the **verified token address only** (`verifiedEmail`, set from a verified ID token whose `email_verified` is true, remembered on the job so token-less cron/admin retries can still use it). Profile, directory and unverified token e-mails are never used. |
| App | Chats shows a "Verify your e-mail for group invitations" banner while the ID token is unverified: "Send e-mail" (60 s resend cooldown) and "I have verified" (reload, then a **forced ID-token refresh**; the token claim decides, never client state). Invitations are subscribed only once verified. Nothing else waits on verification. Copy never claims delivery from a successful request. |
