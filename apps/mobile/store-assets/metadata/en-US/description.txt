SoloTravelSoul is the travel companion built for independent explorers. Whether you're hopping continents solo or escaping for a weekend, SoloTravelSoul keeps every trip beautifully organized from first plan to lasting memory.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY
Build day-by-day itineraries with ease. Add places, notes, and logistics for every day of your trip. See your full trip at a glance and stay organized no matter how complex the adventure.

PACK SMARTER
SoloTravelSoul analyzes your destination, trip length, and itinerary to suggest exactly what to pack — beach gear, hiking essentials, city outfits, international documents. Check items off as you go.

JOURNAL EVERY MOMENT
Write travel memories the moment they happen. Add a mood, tag a place, and attach photos. Your journal organizes entries by day in a clean timeline — each trip becomes a story you can relive anytime.

NEVER MISS A BEAT WITH REMINDERS
Set local reminders so the app notifies you 7 days before, 1 day before, and the morning of your trip. No account needed, no push server — reminders fire straight from your device.

DISCOVER NEW PLACES
Browse a curated collection of destinations. Filter by category — Beach, Hiking, City, Food, Adventure, Nightlife, Hidden Gem, or Solo-friendly — and save your favorites to your personal library.

WORKS OFFLINE
SoloTravelSoul keeps your itinerary, checklist, and journal available even without a signal. Changes sync automatically when you reconnect. Your trip data is never held hostage by a weak airport Wi-Fi.

SHARE YOUR ADVENTURES
Export your full trip as a shareable summary — destination, itinerary, packing progress, journal highlights — and send it to friends or save it for yourself.

YOUR DATA, YOUR MEMORIES
Everything you create belongs to you. SoloTravelSoul uses Firebase with strict security rules so only your account can access your content. No ads. No data selling. No subscriptions.

DESIGNED FOR SOLO TRAVELERS
Clean, distraction-free interface optimized for one thing: making your travel life easier and your memories more vivid.
