SoloTravelSoul collects only what it needs to give you a personalized travel experience:

DATA COLLECTED:
• Email address and name (for your account)
• Trip data you create: destinations, itinerary, journal entries, packing lists
• Travel preferences you set in your profile
• App usage data for crash reporting (if enabled)

DATA NOT COLLECTED:
• No advertising identifiers
• No precise location stored on our servers (location is used on-device for "Near me" sorting only and never sent to our servers)
• No contacts, microphone, or camera data beyond photos you explicitly upload to your journal

HOW YOUR DATA IS USED:
• To sync your trip data securely across your devices via Firebase
• To personalize packing suggestions based on your trip details
• To fire local notifications you request (reminders are scheduled on your device — no push server)

YOUR RIGHTS:
• You can delete your account and all associated data at any time
• We do not sell your data or use it for advertising
• All data is stored in Firebase Firestore with strict per-user security rules

For the full privacy policy, visit the Privacy Policy screen inside the app.
